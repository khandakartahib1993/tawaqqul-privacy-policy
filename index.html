Welcome

Welcome to Tawaqqul.

Tawaqqul is an Islamic companion app that provides Quran, Hadith, duas, Asmaul Husna, prayer times, Qibla, nearby-mosque discovery, Islamic learning content, trackers, calculators, quizzes, Smart Masjid Clock information, a Shop, and related services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the current version of Tawaqqul handles, why it is used, where it is stored, when it is shared with service providers, and the choices available to you.

Tawaqqul is an independent application. It is not an official application of Quran.com, Quran Foundation, OpenStreetMap, Google, Firebase, AlAdhan, or any other third-party service named in this Policy.

01

Privacy Policy

Summary of Our Approach

Most general Islamic features can be used without creating an account. We try to keep data collection limited to what is needed for the feature you choose to use.

General Islamic Features

Reading bundled Quran, Hadith, duas, Asmaul Husna, Nurani Quran, and other included educational content does not require you to provide a name, phone number, or email address. Quran content included in the current app build is stored locally in the app, so ordinary Quran reading does not send your reading activity to Quran.com or Quran Foundation.

Account and Shop

Phone-based login is used for account and Shop-related features. If you sign in, use the Shop, submit feedback, or use a cloud-synced feature, the information described below may be processed.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it to build targeted advertising profiles.

02

Privacy Policy

Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, Tawaqqul may handle:

  • Account information, including your verified phone number, Firebase user identifier, name, optional email address, profile address, and authentication status
  • Shop and transaction information, including saved delivery addresses, ordered items, quantities, selected variants, order totals, discounts, delivery area and charge, selected payment method, order status, coupon use, reviews, product questions, and Shop notifications
  • Feedback information, including feedback type and message, optional contact details, and, when signed in, your account name and phone number
  • Location information, including precise or approximate coordinates when permission is granted, a manually selected location, and a locally cached location name
  • App activity and preferences, including settings, notification choices, Quran bookmarks and last-read position, saved or favorite items, tracker entries, quiz activity, reward points, and locally scheduled reminders
  • Zakat records, including the values, dates, obligations, and transaction entries you choose to enter
  • Technical information needed to operate the app, including platform, app version, Firebase authentication and installation identifiers, Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token, and last-active timestamp for signed-in users

The current app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or general photo and file library.

03

Privacy Policy

Account, Login, Profile, and Shop Data

Tawaqqul uses Firebase Authentication for phone-number verification. Your phone number and authentication information are processed to send and verify a one-time password, maintain your signed-in session, and protect account-only features.

If you create or update a profile, the app may store your name, phone number, optional email, address, Firebase user identifier, saved addresses, and account creation information in Google Cloud Firestore.

When you place an order, Tawaqqul stores the information required to process, deliver, support, and maintain a record of that order. The current app records the selected payment method but does not collect bank-card details, mobile-banking PINs, or payment-account passwords.

Reviews and product questions may include your name, phone number, rating, comment or question, and submission time. These are used to display and manage the relevant Shop feature.

Affiliate products may open an external website or app. Tawaqqul does not control the information collected by that external service.

04

Privacy Policy

Location Information

Tawaqqul may request precise and approximate location access. Location is used only for features that need a geographical position.

Location

The app may obtain your current latitude and longitude from your device after permission is granted. It may also let you select a location manually. The most recent coordinates and location name may be cached on your device so that prayer information can load more quickly and remain partially available offline.

Nearby Mosques and Map

To find nearby mosques, Tawaqqul sends a small geographic bounding box derived from your current coordinates to a public Overpass API server. The server returns nearby OpenStreetMap places. The app then filters and measures distances on your device. When map view is opened, map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap's tile service. Those providers may receive your IP address, request time, user-agent or app identifier, and the requested geographic area as part of normal server operation.

Prayer Times and Geocoding

Your coordinates and date are sent to the AlAdhan API to retrieve prayer times and Hijri date information. When the app converts coordinates into a place name, or a manually entered place name into coordinates, the device's geocoding service may process the location. Depending on your platform, that service may be provided by Google, Apple, or another system provider.

Qibla and Smart Masjid Clock

Your location is used on the device to calculate the Qibla direction and to sort Smart Masjid Clock-enabled mosques retrieved from Firestore by distance.

Tawaqqul does not intentionally save your raw GPS coordinates to its own Firestore database for prayer times, Qibla, or OpenStreetMap nearby-mosque search. However, third-party location services receive the information described above when the relevant feature makes a network request.

You may deny or revoke location permission in your device settings. Prayer times, Qibla, nearby mosques, and Smart Masjid Clock discovery may be unavailable or less accurate without it.

05

Privacy Policy

Local Storage and Cloud Sync

Local-only Data

Tawaqqul uses SharedPreferences, Isar, Firestore offline cache, and other device storage mechanisms. Depending on the feature, locally stored data may include theme and font settings, notification settings, selected or cached location, prayer-time cache, Quran bookmarks and last-read position, Nurani Quran page and bookmarks, Hadith and dua indexes, favorite mosque, Tasbih history, Amal Tracker, Ramadan Tracker, Zakat Tracker cache, and other preferences.

Local information generally remains until the app removes it, you clear app data, or you uninstall the app. Device backups or operating-system features may retain some data according to your device settings.

Cloud-synced Data

If you are signed in, Zakat Tracker records are synchronized to a Firestore subcollection under your account. Quiz points, quiz activity, reward vouchers, profile information, Shop activity, and notification information may also be stored in Firestore. Because Zakat records can reveal financial and religious information, enter and sync only information you are comfortable storing with your account.

Logging out does not automatically delete cloud records or previously submitted orders, reviews, questions, or feedback.

06

Privacy Policy

Feedback and Communications

When you submit feedback, Tawaqqul stores the feedback type, message, optional email or phone number, platform, app version, submission time, and status in Firestore. If you are signed in, the feedback also includes your account name and phone number.

The same feedback details are sent through a Tawaqqul-operated Cloudflare Worker so that an email notification can be delivered through Resend to the app owner. Do not include passwords, payment PINs, private documents, or other unnecessary sensitive information in feedback.

We may use the contact information you voluntarily provide to respond to your message.

07

Privacy Policy

Notifications

Tawaqqul uses local notifications and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Local notifications may be scheduled on your device for prayer times, daily Islamic content, and other reminders. Android permissions for notifications, alarms, wake lock, vibration, and restart handling are used to deliver these reminders reliably and do not by themselves give Tawaqqul access to your personal files.

Firebase Cloud Messaging creates a registration token for the app installation and may subscribe the installation to a general notification topic. If you are signed in, the token and a last-active timestamp are stored with your Firestore customer profile so that account-specific and order notifications can be delivered.

You can disable notifications in Tawaqqul settings or your device settings. Disabling notifications does not prevent you from using the main Islamic reading features.

08

Privacy Policy

Third-Party Services

The following third-party services may process information when you use the relevant feature:

Google Firebase

Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, and Cloud Functions support login, cloud data, Shop operations, feedback storage, and notifications. Google may process device, network, authentication, installation, and service-use information according to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

OpenStreetMap and Overpass API

OpenStreetMap tiles and map data are used to display maps and find nearby mosques. Nearby searches may use public Overpass API mirrors operated by different organizations. Their server logs may include your IP address, request metadata, and requested geographic area. OpenStreetMap information and attribution: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

AlAdhan

AlAdhan receives coordinates and date information when prayer times are requested. Service information: https://aladhan.com/prayer-times-api

Google Maps

Google Maps is not the embedded map used for nearby-mosque browsing. When you tap Directions, Tawaqqul opens a Google Maps directions URL in an external app or browser with the mosque destination coordinates. From that point, Google's terms and privacy policy apply.

Cloudflare and Resend

Feedback is sent through a Cloudflare Worker and may be delivered by Resend as an email notification. These providers may process the submitted feedback and standard network metadata according to their own policies.

Affiliate Sites

External affiliate links, product links, and other external websites are governed by the destination service's own privacy policy. Tawaqqul does not control their data practices.

Privacy Policy

Islamic Content, Trackers, and Sensitive Information

Quran reading progress, Amal Tracker entries, Ramadan Tracker entries, Zakat records, favorite mosques, and similar activity can reveal information about religious practice. Financial values entered into Zakat tools may also be sensitive.

Most such activity is stored only on your device. The main exception in the current build is that Zakat Tracker records and quiz or reward activity are synchronized with Firestore when you are signed in, as described above.

You choose whether to use these features and what information to enter. Do not enter information you do not want stored locally or, where cloud sync applies, associated with your account.

Islamic calculators and educational content are general tools. They are not a substitute for advice from a qualified scholar, mufti, financial professional, lawyer, or other appropriate professional.

Privacy Policy

Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell or rent personal information. The current app does not include an advertising SDK or an analytics SDK and does not use your information for targeted advertising.

Information is shared only when needed for a requested feature, with the service providers described in this Policy, to comply with law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

Public reviews or questions may be visible to other users together with the display information included in that feature. Do not publish information you want to keep private.

Privacy Policy

Data Retention

Local data is generally retained until it is removed by the app, you clear Tawaqqul's storage, or you uninstall the app.

Cloud account, profile, Zakat, quiz, reward, feedback, notification, and Shop information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, satisfy transaction and recordkeeping needs, and comply with applicable law.

Order and transaction records may be retained after account deletion where reasonably required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, warranty, or dispute-resolution purposes. We may retain anonymized or aggregated information that no longer identifies you.

Privacy Policy

Your Choices, Access, Correction, and Deletion

You can:

  • Deny or revoke location permission
  • Disable notifications and exact reminders
  • Use general Islamic content without signing in
  • Edit supported profile information and saved addresses
  • Log out of your account
  • Clear local app data or uninstall the app
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of eligible cloud account data by contacting us

Logging out only ends the signed-in session and clears the app's local account cache. It does not delete cloud records.

To request deletion of your account and associated eligible data, email tawaqqul@aawtechnologies.com with the subject “Tawaqqul Data Deletion Request” and include the verified phone number used for the account. We may ask you to verify account ownership before acting on the request.

Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local data from that device but does not automatically delete Firestore data, submitted feedback, public reviews or questions, or Shop transaction records.

Permissions

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the current service, including Firebase authentication, Firestore security rules, server-side Cloud Functions for privileged messaging tasks, transport encryption for Tawaqqul's fixed service endpoints, and access controls. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device, SIM card, one-time passwords, and account access. Never share an OTP, password, payment PIN, or other secret through Tawaqqul feedback or product questions.

Children's Privacy

Tawaqqul provides general Islamic educational content that families may use, but account, feedback, reward, and Shop features are not directed specifically to children under 13.

We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. Parents or guardians should supervise a child's use of account and Shop features. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

Accuracy of Islamic and Location Information

Prayer times, Qibla direction, mosque listings, Hijri dates, translations, tafsir, Hadith details, calculators, and other content may vary by source, calculation method, location accuracy, scholarly interpretation, or third-party data quality.

We make reasonable efforts to provide useful information, but important religious matters should be verified with qualified scholars or trusted Islamic authorities. Map and mosque information should also be independently confirmed when safety or travel decisions depend on it.
